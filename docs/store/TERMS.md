## What FairShare is

FairShare is an educational calculator. You give it a stock's price history
and the terms of an option contract; it applies three published models to
those inputs and shows you what they produce.

## The models have real limits

The methods FairShare uses — Black-Scholes-Merton, Monte Carlo simulation,
and a Baum-Welch hidden Markov model for volatility regimes — are standard
published techniques with well-known assumptions that markets routinely
violate. In particular:

- They assume the future resembles the recent past. It often does not.
- They are only as good as the price history you provide. Bad or stale data
  produces confident, wrong numbers.
- Probabilities shown are properties of a simulation, not of the world.

**Options can expire worthless, and writing them can lose more than you
receive.** Never trade money you cannot afford to lose. Take your own advice
from someone licensed to give it.
