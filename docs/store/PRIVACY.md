# FairShare Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 22, 2026_

FairShare is an educational calculator for stock-option fair values. It is
designed so that it does not need your data, and so it does not take any.

## Who we are

FairShare is published by Jaivik Patel, an individual developer. Questions
about this policy, or about privacy in the app, can be sent to the contact
address shown on the app's store listing, or raised as an issue on the
FairShare repository at https://github.com/jaivik-2612/vscode_remote.

## What we collect

Nothing. FairShare has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising
SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or
share any personal or sensitive user data, and we do not build profiles of
the people who use the app.

## What stays on your device

Everything you type — ticker searches, price histories you paste or import,
contract settings — is processed entirely on your device and is never
transmitted to us. We operate no servers and receive no telemetry. The
symbol directory and the interest-rate curves the app prices with are
bundled inside the app itself, so looking up a ticker sends no request
anywhere.

## Network access

The app itself makes no network requests. If you choose to follow one of the
data links the app offers (Google Sheets, Stooq, or Yahoo Finance), that
happens in your own browser under those services' own privacy policies;
nothing about it flows back to us.

## Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to retain and nothing
for you to ask us to delete — we hold no record of you at all. The values
you enter live only in the app on your own device for as long as the app is
open; clearing them, or uninstalling the app, removes them completely.

## Security

All computation happens locally on your device, so your inputs never travel
over a network and never reach a server we control. There is no account to
be compromised and no stored database of user data to breach. Anything you
choose to copy out of the app — into a spreadsheet, for example — then falls
under that other service's own protections.

## Third parties

None. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is
collected.

## Children

FairShare is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does
not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy will be
updated first and the change described plainly in the app's release notes.
The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
