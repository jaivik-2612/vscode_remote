      The numbers behind the app

      Every calculation in this app uses the factors below — nothing else. Each activity has an operation factor (the fuel or electricity used while doing it) and an embodied factor (making the equipment, amortised over its lifetime — or for food, the full farm-to-shop production footprint). Values marked ≈ are derived estimates; the rest come from the cited published datasets.

      About the numbers

      Every factor, its source, and whether it is a published value or a derived estimate is listed in the Data tab. Headline sources: UK DEFRA conversion factors (via Our World in Data), Poore & Nemecek 2018 (food), Ember (grid intensity), ICCT/IVL (vehicle manufacturing), IEA (streaming). Good for habit tracking and comparison, not formal carbon accounting. CH₄ and N₂O are shown as CO₂-equivalent (GWP-100).
